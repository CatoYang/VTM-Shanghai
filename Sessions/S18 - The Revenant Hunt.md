**The temple**
We find cats in the temple and fille finds them near a wall.  
One of the cats licks her (and the green blood) and immediately dies. The shadow leaves the cat and it immediately runs into the wall. It is shown that the wall is an illusion and it leads into a lightless cave down into the earth. We find that the tunnels deeper are lined with sigils of the prince, a taowu symbol and one we do not recognise.   
In a wider area we find there are pieces of jade everywhere. It seemed that an explosion occurred in the vicinity and there were claw marks on the wall. Fille finds out in a vision that kindred were in great pain here, and it was recent.
[[Yao Li]] uses clairvoyance and sees a vision of a bronze sword with blood running down it stuck to the earth and was told it was important.

We find [[Wong Ka Ming]] on a slab and [[Zhou Yu]] knealing with a bronze sword in his hand. The sword is impaled onto the earth. 
Their bodies are black and desiccated and we find that on [[Wong Ka Ming]] there are cut marks that match her dagger. 
There was also a ring of blood around them and the floor is cracked and fused.

We hear someone has entered the tunnels and contemplate options

We then decide to push forward. We find a door lined with silver, behind it a large room with a altar and a body draped over it.
The walls are lined with bronze, copper and silver. 

The winds then rush in and coalesce into a shadowy figure that resembles the prince, its face shifting between the prince and a dark entity full of malice.

We descend into a fight and learn that the entity is incorporeal and requires 3 servings of blood per round.
A slight indecisive fight occurs as [[Zhou Haoran]] and [[Fille]] fight the entity while [[Yao Li]], [[He Zhen]] and [[Moi Yan]] goes to the body on the altar whom we learn is the prince and loot it. Finding
- an jade amulet for [[Moi Yan]]
After another round of fighting [[Zhou Haoran]] finally kills the entity and it explodes spraying green and black blood everywhere staining [[Zhou Haoran]] and a black cloud fuses into him.

Post battle looting reveals 
- An ominous obsidian stone for [[He Zhen]]
- a ring for [[Yao Li]] 

We sleep for the night as sun sets.

On the next day we carry the prince's body out and find [[Li Xuan]] on the way out reminiscing of the things that happened here. He tells us to bring the body back to [[Zhao Li Ling]] and will go and inspect the blood sigils in the room that he has forgotten.
He also still expresses interests in the vase that we have not delivered to him.

The scenario ends, the world will want to know what happened.
