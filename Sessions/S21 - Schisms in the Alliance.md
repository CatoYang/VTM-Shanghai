The Coterie splits into two factions, one for [[Zhao Li Ling]] and the other for [[Madam Meixiu Chanel]]. Ventrue vs Toreador.

[[Yao Li]] is tasked with getting familiar with the generals and warlords of shanghai

[[Moi Yan]] joins the council of commerce.

[[Zhou Haoran]] and [[He Zhen]] seizes some of the territory of [[Zhou Yu|Zhou Yu †]] with the help of [[Li Han]] and manages to sway some of the green gang lieutenants.

---

[[Zhao Li Ling]] tasks the ventrue side of the coterie with helping out [[Adriana Bianca]].
[[Moi Yan]], [[Zhou Haoran]] and [[He Zhen]] meet adriana at one of her residences and gives them the job of persuading one of the generals holding shanghai to stop shaking down the merchants working for her.
She tells them that the general has a concubine that is willing to defect and tell them more.

The trio meets with the woman and learn a few things
- The general is deeply superstitious and visits a soothsayer
- He is debauched and has a harem. He parties frequently
- He wears a jade pendant and its important to him
- He has a sword that he practices with daily
- The concubine wants her death to be faked so she can elope.

After a slew of bad ideas.

Moi yan sets him up at a brothel and hires prostitutes to attempt to steal the pendant so that [[Zhou Haoran]] can corrupt it. The plan fails as the prostitutes fail and is beaten by him.
He is outraged at the incident and retreats to a private room. [[He Zhen]] intervenes as a disguised employee of the establishment tries to salvage the situation. [[He Zhen]] attempts to bypass the soldier guarding the room with dominate disciplines and attempts but fails to sway the general. [[He Zhen]] then retreats without the pendant, risking exposure.

[[Zhou Haoran]] attempts to break into the general's mansion while he is away at the brothel to steal his sword and is successful. He then brings it to a swordsmith to sabotage and sneaks back in to return the sword without detection.

[[He Zhen]] then goes to to the general's soothsayer, [[Master Dao]], to subvert him and learn that the soothsayer has genuine foresight and claims that he should have died 2 years ago and that he needs to avoid someone named [[Tang]]. With soporific touch and mesmerise he incepts a message to scare the general into backing off the merchants through superstition and the soothsayer's words.

Days later the sword breaks and the merchants' burden is lifted.

[[Zhou Haoran]] sends green gang men to burn down the concubine's residence with a dead body of adequate proportions to fake her death.

[[Adriana Bianca]] pledges support for [[Zhao Li Ling]]

[[Fille]] is sent to meet with [[Takahashi Renjiro]] by [[Madam Meixiu Chanel]].

[[Takahashi Renjiro]] wants her to conduct a false flag operation and instructs [[Fille]] to  interdict a russian communist cell and assuming one of their agent's identity (Natalyia). Then, disguised as the agent infiltrate a chinese communist cell and help them incite a riot against a cotton mill owned indirectly by [[Wen Yuheng]].

[[Fille]] then goes to the russian cell in zhabei, a hotel and infiltrates the cell claiming to be part of the KMT?. She then kills Natalyia and her bodyguard and steal her credentials and documents.

[[Fille]] then infiltrates the communist cell in zhabei disguised as Natalyia and presents intel for them to persuade them to  protest the wages that were cut by 20%.

[[Madam Meixiu Chanel]] then sends [[Tang]] to [[Yao Li]]'s ballroom and hands her a folder with forged reports advocating for the police to conduct pre-emptive arrests and a forged document claiming that the wage decrease is to squeese the workers.
Her task is to assist in the operation and present the documents to the worker's union. 

[[Yao Li]] and [[Fille]] (with false identities) meet with the union leaders and present the forged documents.

---

[[Yao Li]] tells the ventrue sided coterie trio about the forged documents and plans to incite the riot.

The trio then report this to [[Wen Yuheng]]. He is disappointed with the turn of events and says  that the decrease in wages was unavoidable as a bad harvest resulted in the price of cotton increasing. He says he will send an agent to negotiate with the workers.

[[He Zhen]] reports back to [[Wen Yuheng]] and is instructed to take out the [[Toreador (百花 - Bǎi Huā)]] agents during the rioting claiming that its a great opportunity to deal a blow to the opposing faction and that the riot and damage to the factory is a small price to pay for it. He will send men to deal with the thugs but wants the agents 'taken out'.
