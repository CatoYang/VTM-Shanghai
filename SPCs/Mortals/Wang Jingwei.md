**Historical Note**
After Sun Yat-sen died in 1925, Wang Jingwei was widely considered his political heir. He was Sun’s close confidant and was actually the person who **drafted Sun Yat-sen’s last will and testament**. He became the Chairman of the Nationalist Government immediately following Sun's death, though he soon became embroiled in a fierce power struggle with **Chiang Kai-shek**
