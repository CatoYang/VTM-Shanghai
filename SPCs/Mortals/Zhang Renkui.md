![[Pasted image 20251027164207.png]]


Zhang Renkui (張仁奎 / 张仁奎, _Zhāng Rénkuí_) (1859-1944) born in 1859 in Teng County, Shandong province. Zhang was the most prestigious Green Gang leader of the beginning of the 1920s in Shanghai. A former lieutenant of Xu Baoshan, well-connected to warlord politics, his influence extended as far Subei, southern Shandong and Jiangnan. Member of the _Xing Wu Liu_ Branch, it is estimated that 40% of all members of the Tong generational status were Zhang Renkui’s followers.
