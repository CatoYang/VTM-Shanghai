#S23 #S24 

Deputy director of commerce and trade in the shanghai municipal council.

Was coerced by a man with in his 40s (presumed to be [[Hong Yee]]) to be a informant for him. 
He is in a childless marriage and has a mistress [[May|Miss May]] whom is held under watch with threats to them if he does not comply.

After the mistress was safe away from shanghai, he was no longer coerced and his loyalties swapped over to the [[Toreador (百花 - Bǎi Huā)]].
